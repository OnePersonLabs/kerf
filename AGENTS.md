# Working on Kerf

Keep conceptual integrity and code as a byproduct at the center of the product.
Treat the user's purpose as the objective; reconsider mechanisms when their cost outweighs their use.

Use readable source, Markdown, descriptive names, and formatted JSON. Keep derived indexes disposable.
Ordinary targeted operations must not load the whole conceptual model or create a whole-project TypeScript program.
Code observations are evidence; they do not author conceptual relationships.

Implement directly, then verify the affected public behavior. No mandatory TDD, coverage quota, test-per-helper rule, or inherited Projector obligations.
Keep the small workflow checks fast and stop once the requested behavior works.
